# Your own AI

The Nextcloud agent works only with your organisation's Fadenstack server. It has no setting to connect an AI
service of your own, such as a model on your computer or an account with an AI provider.

## Why

The agent works with your organisation's files, under your organisation's rules. Your organisation's Fadenstack
server decides which model answers, checks each tool call before it runs, keeps the records your organisation
needs, and signs the marks that show what was made by AI. Without that server, the agent does not start.

## What you can do instead

- **Ask your administrator** for access to the agent on your organisation's server: see [Get started](get-started.md).
- **Fadenstack's other agents** can work with an AI of your own: the browser agent, the Office add-ins and the desktop
  app. See [Browser agent](../chrome/index.md), [Office add-ins](../office/index.md) and
  [Desktop app](../desktop/index.md).
